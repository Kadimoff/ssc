## Non-negotiable claim policy

- Illustrative demo names and numbers are never evidence.
- A logo, conversation, or expression of interest is not a partnership.
- A draft agreement is not an active agreement.
- A recorded contribution is not delivered until verified.
- An outcome without verified evidence is excluded from quantified claims.
- The internal readiness score is never described as a QS score or external rating.
- Partners receive a correction window for claims attributed to them.

## Suggested submission narrative

### Problem

Student entrepreneurship communities often report activity but cannot consistently show how institutions, mentors, companies, and programs contributed to verified outcomes. Fragmented spreadsheets, informal introductions, and founder-centric storytelling make partnership value difficult to govern or reproduce.

### Innovation

SSC’s Partnership OS treats the partnership—not a social feed—as the primary operating object. Organizations, agreements, program roles, contributions, evidence, outcomes, consent, and audit history share one traceable model.

### Implementation

Describe only the pilot that actually occurred. State the participant scope, partner roles, program duration, evidence rules, verifier separation, and correction process. Include an architecture diagram and a redacted workflow export.

### Evidence and impact

Use verified-only counts. Explain the denominator, measurement window, attribution mode, and known limitations. Separate delivered contributions from outcomes and outputs from longer-term impact.

### Scalability

Explain how program templates, stable entity IDs, role controls, Docker deployment, and versioned methodology allow another campus to adopt the workflow while retaining its own governance and data boundaries.

### Ethics and inclusion

Cover data minimization, consent, partner visibility, correction rights, least privilege, evidence retention, safeguarding, accessibility, and the decision not to store raw national identifiers.

## Five-part delivery record

1. **Research and architecture:** source research preserved; polished DOCX and architecture contract produced.
2. **Partner operations:** organization registry, verification, agreements, lifecycle, and audit implemented.
3. **Joint delivery:** programs, partner roles, cohorts, contributions, outcomes, and role-aware interface implemented.
4. **Production foundation:** Dockerized Bun/Hono/PostgreSQL/Redis/MinIO/Mailpit stack, authentication, RBAC, evidence, reports, and migrations implemented.
5. **Pilot and submission controls:** runbook, verified-only reporting, claim policy, evidence register, and submission checklist implemented.

Items marked “not yet claimable” deliberately remain incomplete until real, authorized pilot evidence exists. Product completion cannot manufacture external validation.
